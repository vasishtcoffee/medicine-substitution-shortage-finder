--- cte_pharmacy_stock_summary ---
purpose: Per-pharmacy stock totals and low-stock counts, then ranked by risk (most low items first).
why: ordinary (non-recursive) CTE feeding a window function RANK().
expected: Three rows; Lakeside ranks 1 (6 low/out items, 132 units), Green Cross 2, City Care 3.
> WITH cte_pharmacy_stock_summary AS ( SELECT p.id, p.name, COUNT(*) AS items, SUM(i.quantity) AS total_units, SUM(i.quantity <= i.reorder_level) AS low_items FRO
  pharmacy | items | total_units | low_items | risk_rank
  Lakeside Pharmacy | 9 | 132 | 6 | 1
  Green Cross Pharmacy | 13 | 327 | 5 | 2
  City Care Pharmacy | 15 | 653 | 5 | 3
