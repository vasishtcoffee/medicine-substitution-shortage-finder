--- q_nested_no_stock ---
purpose: Active medicines that no pharmacy currently has in stock (shortage candidates).
why: NESTED subquery - the inner SELECT runs once, independently of the outer query (NOT IN).
expected: 'Febrix 500 Tablet' and 'Metformin 500 mg Tablet (Brand B)'.
> SELECT m.id, m.name, m.brand FROM medicines m WHERE m.is_active = 1 AND m.id NOT IN (SELECT medicine_id FROM inventory WHERE quantity > 0) ORDER BY m.name
  id | name | brand
  3 | Febrix 500 Tablet | Nova Pharma
  16 | Metformin 500 mg Tablet (Brand B) | Nova Pharma

--- q_recursive_category_descendants ---
purpose: Every descendant of the 'Analgesics' category with its depth and full path.
why: RECURSIVE CTE - the anchor member selects the root, the recursive member joins children until none are left.
expected: Antipyretic analgesics (depth 1) and NSAIDs (depth 1) under Analgesics.
> WITH RECURSIVE descendants AS ( SELECT id, name, parent_id, 0 AS depth, CAST(name AS CHAR(500)) AS path FROM medicine_categories WHERE name = 'Analgesics' UNION
  id | name | depth | path
  2 | Analgesics | 0 | Analgesics
  3 | Antipyretic analgesics | 1 | Analgesics > Antipyretic analgesics
  4 | NSAIDs | 1 | Analgesics > NSAIDs

--- q_group_having_low_items ---
purpose: Pharmacies with at least 3 medicines at or below their reorder level.
why: aggregation with GROUP BY ... HAVING (the filter is on the aggregate, which WHERE cannot do).
expected: Lakeside (6), City Care (5), Green Cross (5).
> SELECT p.name AS pharmacy, COUNT(*) AS low_or_out_items, SUM(i.quantity) AS units_on_hand FROM inventory i JOIN pharmacies p ON p.id = i.pharmacy_id WHERE i.qua
  pharmacy | low_or_out_items | units_on_hand
  Lakeside Pharmacy | 6 | 15
  City Care Pharmacy | 5 | 23
  Green Cross Pharmacy | 5 | 5

--- q_open_shortages_with_stock ---
purpose: Medicines with an OPEN shortage entry and how many units remain across all pharmacies.
why: LEFT JOIN + aggregate so medicines with no inventory rows still appear (with 0).
expected: Febrix 500 Tablet (0 units) and Amoxyl 500 Capsule (30 units).
> SELECT m.name AS medicine, s.severity, s.reported_at, COALESCE(SUM(i.quantity), 0) AS units_all_pharmacies FROM shortage_entries s JOIN medicines m ON m.id = s.
  medicine | severity | reported_at | units_all_pharmacies
  Febrix 500 Tablet | HIGH | 2026-09-20 09:00:00 | 0
  Amoxyl 500 Capsule | MEDIUM | 2026-10-01 10:30:00 | 30
